# FireMind Demo Script

Target length: 90–150 seconds.

1. Launch FireMind and show the home screen with the large Ask FireMind action and quick mood row.
2. Select **Ask FireMind** with the remote.
3. Choose **Mind-bending sci-fi** or enter “I want a mind-bending sci-fi movie under two hours.”
4. Show the short recommendation list and the explanation that grounds each match in genre, mood, and runtime.
5. Open **Moon** or **Arrival**.
6. Show metadata, the Why FireMind explanation, and the spoiler-safe summary.
7. Select **Add to Watchlist**.
8. Open Watchlist and show that the title persists locally.
9. Return home and select a second mood such as Family.
10. Close with: “FireMind turns Fire TV from a content browser into an intelligent viewing companion.”

Do not claim a live Bedrock integration until an actual secured backend deployment is connected and demonstrated.
